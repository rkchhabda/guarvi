          How the score works

          Cross-sectional ranking

          Every feature is ranked across today's NIFTY 100 universe so scores are relative, not absolute. Top-decile = 90-100, bottom-decile = 10-20.

          AI model

          XGBoost classifier (150 trees, depth 4, lr 0.05) trained on a walk-forward schedule. Walk-forward AUC 0.5253, accuracy 51.85%.

          Six factor blend

          Overall score = weighted blend of AI, technical, fundamental, momentum, risk, and valuation. Each factor is 0-100 with equal default weight.

          Guarvi · NIFTY 100 directional-signal research portal.
          Research/education tool only. The directional signal is small (~2 bps/day gross) and not profitable net of Indian retail costs; naive equal-weight NIFTY 100 has historically been the better strategy. Not SEBI-registered investment advice.
